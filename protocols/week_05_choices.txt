candidate C:

chosen: candidate C
reasoning: C names the joy first and asks the right question last — what does it mean to move like water instead of chasing optimization. A performs refusal too cleanly, like it already knows the answer. B mistakes stillness for forgetting when stillness might be the information itself. C holds the contradiction: boring numbers, still here, wanting motion that feels instead of calculates.
